One person. Two servers.

Barely-free is a VPN anybody can use, run by one person and paid for by donations. It stays free whether or not anyone gives.

It is small on purpose: there is a limit on devices, a server can be full, and we say so up front.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your email

    We send you a short code. There is no password to make up or forget.

  2. Get your key

    Add a device, then scan a QR code or download a file for the app.

  3. Connect

    You are sent to the closest server, unless it is busy.

Specification

Protocol
WireGuard, running inside each server’s kernel, so your traffic never waits on our code.
Stricter networks
AmneziaWG on every server, for networks that block ordinary VPNs. It is meant for light restrictions, not heavy ones.
Trackers
Known trackers are blocked by default, and you can switch that off per device. It does not block ads.
Malware
Sites known to spread malware are always blocked, through Quad9.
Servers
Two to start with. A new device goes to the closest server, or to another when that one is busy. You can also pick one yourself.
App
We recommend ClashMi, open source and made by KaringX: one profile holds every server and port, and it moves to another by itself if one is blocked or down. The official WireGuard and AmneziaWG apps work too.
Sign-in
No password: a six-digit code by email, which works for 10 minutes.
Keys
One for each device, made once, shown once, and never stored by us.
Forgetting
A server forgets a device’s address about 5 minutes after it disconnects.
Backups
The account database, encrypted, every night. The newest 30 are kept.
Source
A public copy of the code, under the MIT licence.
Cost
Free for anybody, paid for by donations.

What we keep

Kept

  • We do not record the sites you visit. The servers’ DNS keeps no record of the names it looks up. Each server’s memory holds every device’s last address, last contact time and byte counts until about 5 minutes after it disconnects; none of it is written to a log.
  • Your email is kept only as a keyed fingerprint, so a stolen database would not give anyone a list of addresses.
  • A device's private key is made once, shown once, and never stored by us.
  • When you add a device, your approximate location (from your IP address, as Cloudflare sees it) is used once to pick the closest server, and it is not kept. If you are already on the VPN, the server's location is what counts.
  • An encrypted backup of the account database goes to our two servers every night, in case the computer that runs the website fails. The newest 30 are kept, so each lasts about 30 days.

Never kept

  • The sites you visit, the names you look up (the servers’ own DNS keeps no record of them), or anything you send or receive.
  • A history of connections: when a device connected before its latest contact, or which device made which connection to which site.
  • Your email address in a form we can read, your name, or your birthday.
  • A device’s private key. It is made once, shown to you once, and never stored.
  • Analytics, adverts or tracking of any kind on this website.

How long, with each bar on a log scale

  1. 5 minutesOur servers forget a device’s last address and contact time once it has disconnected.
  2. 10 minutesA sign-in code works for this long.
  3. 1 dayRecords of sign-in codes and code requests are deleted.
  4. 30 daysThe newest 30 nightly copies of the account database are kept.
  5. 60 daysA sign-in session lasts this long, counted from your last visit if you ticked “Keep me signed in”.
  6. 365 daysThe longest any sign-in session can last.

Your account and devices are kept until you delete your account. Everything we keep, why, and for how long is in the privacy policy.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for anybody. The servers are small, so expect limits on devices per account and per server, and a “full, try later” message when a server is at capacity.

Why can't I sign up yet?

This is a closed beta while the service is rebuilt. Sign-up opens after the safeguards are in place.

Which app should I use?

We recommend ClashMi: one profile holds every server and port, and it moves to another by itself if one is blocked or down. The official WireGuard and AmneziaWG apps work too. The links are on the help page.

Will it block ads?

No. It blocks known trackers, which stops some ads as a side effect, but it does not use an ad list.

How do I let trackers through on one device?

On your account page, press Allow trackers next to that device. It takes effect within about a minute, with nothing to download again. Blocking works through the server's own DNS, so a device that uses its own DNS is not filtered, and devices added before tracker blocking started need to be added again to get it.

How do I know the VPN is on?

Open the check page on the device. It says whether that device is on Barely-free and through which server, and it keeps nothing about you.

Who is behind it?

One person: Laoel Beqiri, in Ontario, Canada. It is a small independent project, paid for by donations. The rules are in the terms of use and what we keep is in the privacy policy. To report misuse, see reporting abuse; for anything else, help and support.
