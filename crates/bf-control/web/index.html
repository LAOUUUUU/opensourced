How it works

  1. Sign in with your email

    We send you a short code. There is no password to make up or forget.

  2. Get your key

    Add a device, then scan a QR code or download a file for the WireGuard app.

  3. Connect

    You are sent to the closest server, unless it is busy.

Built for speed and quiet

  • WireGuard

    The fast, modern VPN protocol, running inside each server's kernel so your traffic never waits on our code.

  • Trackers blocked

    Known trackers are blocked by default, and you can switch that off per device. It does not block ads. Sites known to spread malware are always blocked.

  • Closest server

    New devices are sent to the nearest server, and to another one when it is overloaded. You can also pick a server yourself.

  • For stricter networks

    One extra option for networks that block ordinary VPNs. It is meant for light restrictions, not heavy ones.

What we keep

  • We do not record the sites you visit. The servers’ DNS keeps no record of the names it looks up. Each server’s memory holds every device’s last address, last contact time and byte counts until about 5 minutes after it disconnects; none of it is written to a log.
  • Your email is kept only as a keyed fingerprint, so a stolen database would not give anyone a list of addresses.
  • A device's private key is made once, shown once, and never stored by us.
  • When you add a device, your approximate location (from your IP address, as Cloudflare sees it) is used once to pick the closest server, and it is not kept. If you are already on the VPN, the server's location is what counts.
  • An encrypted backup of the account database goes to our two servers every night, in case the computer that runs the website fails. The newest 30 are kept, so each lasts about 30 days.

Everything we keep, why, and for how long is in the privacy policy.

Costs and funding

Barely-free is free, and it is paid for by donations. What people give goes to the servers: keeping the two we have running, and buying more as the money allows, so more people can be let in. The service stays free whether or not anyone gives.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes, for anybody. The servers are small, so expect limits on devices per account and per server, and a “full, try later” message when a server is at capacity.

Why can't I sign up yet?

This is a closed beta while the service is rebuilt. Sign-up opens after the safeguards are in place.

Will it block ads?

No. It blocks known trackers, which stops some ads as a side effect, but it does not use an ad list.

How do I let trackers through on one device?

On your account page, press Allow trackers next to that device. It takes effect within about a minute, with nothing to download again. Blocking works through the server's own DNS, so a device that uses its own DNS is not filtered, and devices added before tracker blocking started need to be added again to get it.

How do I know the VPN is on?

Open the check page on the device. It says whether that device is on Barely-free and through which server, and it keeps nothing about you.
