Report a security problem

Last updated 14 November 2026

If you find a security problem in Barely-free, please tell us privately first, so we can fix it before anyone can misuse it. Use GitHub’s private form for our source code: report a vulnerability. It needs a free GitHub account, and only you and we can read what you send. Never post it anywhere others can read it, such as a public issue. Every other way to reach us is on the help page.

What is in scope

  • This website, barely-free.com: signing in, the account pages and the device setups they make.
  • Our VPN servers, as reached through a Barely-free setup: for example one device reaching another, traffic or DNS leaking, or getting around a kick.
  • The source code we publish on GitHub.

What is not

  • Denial of service and load testing.
  • Spam, phishing or tricking us or our users.
  • Scanner output with no real effect shown.
  • Problems in Cloudflare, Resend, Ko-fi, our hosting company, or the WireGuard and AmneziaWG apps: please report those to them.

Please

  • Use only your own account and devices. Do not read, change or delete anyone else’s data; if you reach some by accident, stop and tell us what you saw.
  • Do not slow down or interrupt the service for anyone else.
  • Give us 90 days to fix the problem before you publish it, or tell us why you think that is too long.

What we promise

  • We will reply within 7 days and keep you told what we are doing about it.
  • If you follow this page in good faith, we will not take legal action against you or report you for your research.
  • We will thank you on this page once the fix is out, if you want.
  • There is no reward. Barely-free has no money beyond donations for its servers.

What to send

What the problem is, how to make it happen, and what someone could do with it. Plain text is best. Our security.txt points here.
