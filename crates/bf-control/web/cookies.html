Cookies

This site can set three cookies, all its own. None of them follows you around the web, and nothing on the site comes from anywhere else: no analytics, no adverts, no embedded content.

__Host-bf_session

What it does: keeps you signed in.

Set: when you type a sign-in code that works. Signing out removes it.

Lasts: 60 days. If you tick “Keep me signed in on this device”, 60 days from the last day you used the site, and never more than a year from when you signed in.

__Host-bf_pending

What it does: remembers which email address you asked for a code for, between the page where you type the address and the page where you type the code. It holds that address and nothing else.

Set: when you ask for a sign-in code. It is removed when you sign in.

Lasts: 10 minutes, as long as the code works.

__Host-bf_theme

What it does: remembers whether you chose Light or Dark at the bottom of a page.

Set: when you press Light or Dark. Pressing System removes it.

Lasts: one year.

Who can see them

All three are sent only over HTTPS and only to this site, and no script can read them (the site runs no scripts). The two sign-in cookies are never sent with a request that starts on another site. Cloudflare, which carries the site’s traffic, sees them as it sees every request.

Removing them

Sign out, press System at the bottom of any page, or clear this site’s cookies in your browser. The site works without any of them; you will just be signed out and see your device’s colours.
