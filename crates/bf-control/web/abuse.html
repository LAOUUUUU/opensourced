Report abuse

Last updated 12 November 2026

If something harmful came from one of our servers, tell us and we will look into it. How to reach us is on the help page.

What to send

  • The IP address of our server that the traffic came from.
  • The date and time, with the time zone (UTC is best).
  • The address and port it reached on your side.
  • What happened, with log lines if you have them.

Plain text is best. We do not open files we were not expecting.

What we can do

  • Block the address or port that is being attacked, for everyone on that server.
  • Limit or block a kind of traffic on a server.
  • Switch off a device, or remove an account, when the report or other facts tell us which one it is.
  • Stop new sign-ups for a while.

We read every report. Barely-free is run by one person, so please allow a few days for a reply; anything urgent is dealt with first.

What we cannot do

All the devices on a server reach the internet from the server’s one address, and we keep no record of which device used which connection. So we cannot tell from an address and a time who was behind it, and we cannot give that to anyone. What we keep is in the privacy policy.

Copyright notices

Send copyright notices, including notices under section 41.25 of Canada’s Copyright Act and DMCA notices from the United States, the same way. Sharing material that infringes copyright breaks our terms.

A notice names one of our addresses and a time. For the reason above, we cannot match it to a person, so we cannot pass it on to anyone, and we will tell you so. When someone who infringes again and again can be identified in some other way, they lose access to Barely-free.

If your device was switched off

Contact us and say which device. We will look again.

Police and courts

How we deal with legal requests is in the privacy policy. The number we receive is on the transparency page.
