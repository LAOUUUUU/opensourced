Counted since sign-up opened to everyone on 12 November 2026. Legal requests are orders or requests from police, courts or government bodies.

What we could hand over

Only if a court order or warrant valid in Canada made us, and only for the accounts it names. We can find an account from its email address, from a device’s public key or its address inside the VPN, and from the IP address a device last connected from while a server still holds it, which is until about 5 minutes after the device disconnects. For such an account: its devices (their names, public keys and addresses inside the VPN, and when they were added and removed), any kicks, and each device’s last contact time and last IP address while a server holds them. The full list is in the privacy policy.

What we could not hand over

  • The sites anyone visited, the names they looked up, or what they sent.
  • Which device made a given connection to a site, or a history of who connected when.
  • A list of email addresses: we keep only fingerprints, so we can check an address someone gives us, but we cannot read any back.
  • A device’s private key: we never keep it.

Why there is no warrant canary

Some services publish a signed statement every month saying they have had no secret orders, and stop publishing it if they get one. We do not: it needs a signing key and an update every month, and a missed update would alarm people for nothing. Instead, every request we are allowed to count is in the table above.
